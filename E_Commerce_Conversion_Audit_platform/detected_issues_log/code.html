Heuristic & Technical Breakdown

User testing and session replays reveal that mandatory login accounts prior to entering billing information introduce severe customer friction. 57% of first-time shoppers abandon the funnel immediately upon seeing this barrier rather than utilizing a single-click Guest Checkout alternative.

lightbulb Recommended Optimization Strategy

Implement 1-Click Guest Checkout with progressive account creation on the Order Confirmation page. Pre-populate account details using the shipping email and offer frictionless password set-up after payment validation.
